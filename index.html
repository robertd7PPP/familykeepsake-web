The pile

You can't keep it all. And you can't bin it.

Every week they bring home more: a painting still wet at the corners, a swimming certificate, a Lego spaceship you're not allowed to touch. Some of it makes the fridge door. Most of it goes into a bag in a cupboard, waiting for a wet Sunday that never comes.

Photographing it should be the answer - but those photos sink into a camera roll full of screenshots and receipts, unnamed, undated, unfindable. Keeping the memory shouldn't mean typing out who-what-when three hundred times.

FamilyKeepsake makes the photo do the work.

  • ✓ Capture it, review the details and save a named file
  • ✓ The painting can leave; the keepsake stays
  • ✓ Recognised words and saved details become searchable
  • ✓ The whole collection plays back in Rewind

Your archive

An archive that outlives the app.

Every keepsake is saved as an ordinary photo or PDF with a readable name, foldered by category, in your family's own iCloud Drive. Open them in Files. Copy them anywhere. If FamilyKeepsake vanished tomorrow, your kids' childhood would still be sitting there - organised.

  • ✓ Real files, readable names - no export required, ever
  • ✓ Details are also saved as JSON files in a hidden archive folder
  • ✓ Syncs between supported iPhones, iPads and Macs through your iCloud; the ordinary files also open in Files or Finder
  • ✓ Delete the app; keep everything
Find

Search the words you saved.

Search saved descriptions, names, years and notes, plus text recognised on the creation. Combine words such as "Kaylee swimming certificate" to find keepsakes whose saved information contains each word.

  • ✓Words on it - every certificate, report and scribbled title is read and kept
  • ✓Words about it - descriptions and your own notes
  • ✓ By kid, by year, by type - or all three at once
  • ✓ Edit the details yourself, with optional AI suggestions at capture
Rewind

Flick back through the years.

Rewind puts the whole collection on a dark stage - newest at the front, the past receding behind. Flick, and a school term flies by. Cross into an earlier year and it announces itself, full screen. It's the shoebox moment, minus the attic.

  • ✓ One flick = one step back in time
  • ✓ A year rail jumps you straight to 2022
  • ✓ Scope to one kid and watch just their story
  • ✓ Or press play and let it drift
Voice notes

Their story, in their voice.

Tap the mic while you save and let them explain it. The recording is kept beside the artwork in your archive. When on-device speech transcription is available, its words become searchable too. In ten years, that voice will be worth more than the painting.

  • ✓ On-device transcription when available; editable text
  • ✓ The audio file lives beside the artwork, forever
  • ✓ Say it once - find it by those words years later

Privacy

Your kids' things never reach us.

Many keepsake services upload your children's work to their own cloud. FamilyKeepsake is built the other way around: optional AI reads on your device, saved files sync through your private iCloud, and there is no FamilyKeepsake-operated cloud at all.

No cloud AI

Nothing is uploaded for AI processing. Capture and reading work in flight mode.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no password. The app never asks who you are.

Your iCloud, not ours

Files and sync ride on the Apple Account your family already owns.

No analytics, no ads

No developer analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing is tracked, measured or sold.

No subscription

There are no servers, so there's nothing for a monthly fee to fund.

About things, not faces

It catalogues what your kids make - the artefacts, not the children.

Why we can promise this

FamilyKeepsake has no servers. Optional AI runs on your device; storage and sync use the private iCloud attached to your Apple Account. We cannot see, receive or hold your family's content - and that isn't a promise resting on a future policy. It's the architecture.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Where are my kids' keepsakes stored?

In your family's own iCloud Drive, as ordinary photos and PDFs with readable names, organised into folders by category. FamilyKeepsake has no servers - we couldn't hold your photos if we wanted to.

Does the AI upload my photos anywhere?

No photo is uploaded for AI processing. Optional Apple Intelligence runs on your supported device and works in flight mode; you can also add captions and details manually. FamilyKeepsake sends nothing to us; saved files sync only through your private iCloud.

What happens if I stop using FamilyKeepsake?

Everything stays. Your keepsakes are ordinary files in your own iCloud Drive - named, foldered, and readable by any app. The archive outlives the app, by design.

Is it a subscription?

No - and it never will be. Capturing one creation at a time is free forever. A single one-time purchase unlocks bulk imports, PDF imports, share-sheet saves and Yearbooks. Your local price is shown in the App Store.

What's free, and what does the unlock buy?

Free forever: capture with the camera or document scanner, photos of one creation, parent captions, manual edits, optional on-device AI reading, unlimited keepsakes, search, gallery filters, Rewind and voice notes. The one-time unlock adds bulk Photos import, PDF import, share-sheet saves and print-ready Yearbooks, and is eligible for Apple Family Sharing.

Which devices does it need?

FamilyKeepsake works on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 27, and on compatible Apple silicon Macs with macOS 27. Apple Intelligence is optional: you can capture keepsakes, add parent captions, edit details and organise your collection manually. On supported devices, Apple Intelligence makes capture and organisation easier with on-device image understanding, suggested descriptions and metadata.

How does it know which of my kids made something?

You can choose the child manually. When Apple Intelligence is available, it can read a signed name and suggest a match against your family. You review the selection before saving. A joint creation can belong to more than one child.

What about school reports?

Scan them from the app for free, or use the one-time unlock to import a multi-page PDF. The PDF is archived in your iCloud Drive. Text recognition runs across its pages for search; optional Apple Intelligence uses the first page and recognised text to suggest details. The school can come from your family's dated school timeline.

Where do my Yearbooks go?

Each finished Yearbook is a standard PDF saved to iCloud Drive › FamilyKeepsake › Yearbooks. Open it again from the app, share it anywhere, print it at home or take it to any print shop.

What if it reads something wrong?

You always see the card before it saves, and every field can be fixed with a tap. Filenames use the child, creation type and date shown on the review card, including any changes you make.
