Quick answers

  • Where are my files? Open the Files app → iCloud Drive → FamilyKeepsake. Every keepsake is there as an ordinary photo or PDF, foldered by category. (In the app: Settings → Your archive → Show in Files.)
  • The AI says it's unavailable. FamilyKeepsake reads with Apple Intelligence, which must be enabled in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, on a supported device (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an M-series iPad). You can always fill in the card by hand.
  • A save failed saying storage is full. Your device's storage has run out — free some space and tap Save again. Nothing you captured is lost while the review card is open.
  • Sync between devices isn't happening. Check both devices are signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled, then give it a few minutes — iCloud sets its own pace.
  • Restore a purchase. Settings (in the app) → Back-catalogue unlock → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
