What do Low, Medium and High mean?

These are indicative paid-access bands using US reference tiers: Low is US$20 or less, Medium is above US$20 through US$60, and High is above US$60. “Once” is a one-time payment; “/ yr” is a year of subscription. Where a comparable annual US reference was not established, twelve monthly payments set the band. Local prices, taxes and offers can differ.

Free access, paid feature gates and print orders are shown separately. Plans can include different features; see each app note for the reference tier and limits. We leave the cost Unclear where the current price or offer could not be established.

From the founder

Why I built FamilyKeepsake.

I wanted the kids’ art in the iCloud storage I already paid for. I wanted to capture it easily, organise it properly and find it again when I needed it.

Taking a photo was only part of that. I wanted a description and useful details to come with it, so a creation could be found later by what it was, who made it or the words on it. Apple Intelligence’s image understanding in iOS 27 made that combination possible on the device.

I also wanted one creation to hold several photos. A craft project or a Lego build can need more than one angle. Reports and certificates mattered too: I wanted those alongside the things the kids made, with the details that help them make sense years later.

Those choices shaped FamilyKeepsake. You can add your own captions and edit the details manually. When Apple Intelligence is available, it helps by understanding the image and suggesting a description and metadata. It makes capturing and organising easier, but it is optional. The saved information is searchable, and the actual photos and documents sit in your own iCloud Drive as ordinary files.

I wanted the payment model to suit that approach. Capturing one creation at a time—including several photos—is free, along with parent captions, manual edits, search, gallery filters, Rewind, voice notes and optional AI reading. One purchase adds bulk imports, PDF imports, share-sheet saves and printable PDF Yearbooks. There is no subscription.

The aim is a useful home for what the kids create: easy to add to, easy to find things in, and stored in the iCloud account you already use. You can use it on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

01FamilyKeepsakeArtwork & creations+

Unlimited one-creation capture is free: camera, document scans and several photos of one creation. Parents can add captions and edit descriptions, child, date and creation type manually. Search, gallery filters, Rewind and voice notes help find saved work. Optional on-device Apple Intelligence adds image understanding, suggested descriptions and metadata; the app remains usable without it.

Bulk Photos import, PDF import, share-sheet saves and printable PDF Yearbooks use one non-consumable unlock. No subscription. Available on iPhone, iPad and compatible Apple silicon Macs, with iOS/iPadOS/macOS 27. Apple Intelligence has separate hardware and model-availability requirements.

Saved files use your own iCloud Drive; Apple storage costs can apply. Photos are JPEG-encoded, and metadata is also written as JSON in a hidden archive folder. A complete archive-restoration workflow and cross-Apple-Account in-app collaboration were not established. For a multi-photo creation or PDF, AI interprets the first image with recognised text as context; text recognition runs across the pages for search. Voice transcription depends on speech availability; the recording is kept if transcription is unavailable.
